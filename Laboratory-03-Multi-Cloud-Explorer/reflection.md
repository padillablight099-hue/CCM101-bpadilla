# Mission Reflection

## Which cloud platform impressed you the most?
Amazon Web Services impressed me the most because of its vast global infrastructure and extremely wide range of services. With 39 regions and over 200 products, it offers the most comprehensive set of tools for any type of workload. The documentation is thorough, the console is well-organized, and it has the longest track record of reliability and innovation.

## What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
All three platforms share the same fundamental structure. They all offer virtual machines, object storage, virtual networking, identity management, and managed databases. They all organize infrastructure into Regions and Availability Zones, use pay-as-you-go pricing, and provide web-based management consoles. The core concepts are nearly identical — only the service names and interfaces differ.

## How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?
Choosing a cloud platform depends entirely on what the business actually needs. Startups care about free tiers and low cost; Microsoft-focused organizations choose Azure for compatibility; AI research teams pick GCP for its specialized tools; and global enterprises choose AWS for broad coverage. Technical preference matters less than matching the platform's strengths to the business goals.

## What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?
I learned that recommending a cloud platform is not about picking the most popular one — it is about understanding the client's situation, existing systems, and future goals. Each platform has advantages, and the best recommendation is the one that fits the client's needs perfectly, even if it is not my personal favorite.

## How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved?
My portfolio now includes structured research on all three major cloud providers, clear comparison tables, practical client recommendations, and real evidence from screenshots. Everything is organized in one place with proper formatting, making it easy to review and reuse. It shows not just what I read, but what I understand and can explain.
